Roundabout

Art that turns with your record.

A record player with collectible toppers. When the side ends, the topper keeps the music going.

How it works

  1. 1

    Place a topper

    It tells the player what it is. No app, no account.

  2. 2

    Play your record

    The scene turns with the record, one turn every 1.8 seconds. No motor in the topper.

  3. 3

    The side ends, the music doesn't

    Each topper carries about an hour of music. No subscription.

More toppers

The nine toppers above are only the first ideas. A topper can be any scene that looks good turning once every 1.8 seconds, in any season. Here are a few more we are thinking about.
